Every machine is an island

Run the daemon on a laptop, a desktop, the cloud, or the box in the closet. Each one becomes an island circling your citadel. Agents and sessions survive island restart.

Up and ready in two commands

Start with a world on your own machine, register a repository, and give an agent work from the app.

STEP 1

Raise a world

One command starts everything a single machine needs and opens the world in your browser.

skylands devworld up
STEP 2

Register a repository

The daemon snapshots its tracked files. A terrace rises on the island with a building for each one.

skylands repo register ~/code/storefront
STEP 3

Give an agent work

Dispatch work directly or let Skylands decide where the work goes. An airship takes off and gets to work.

Explore your agentic world

The world isn't just a pretty interface. Every shape is a simple representation of a concept you'd normally have to use a terminal to interact with: who's working, on what, and what's waiting on me.

One binary, two roles

A daemon turns the machine it runs on into an island in your world. A citadel serves the web app for all of them.

skylands daemon up

The island

Runs on each machine that hosts agents. It launches them, keeps their conversations, applies your rules, and keeps working when the network drops, catching the world up when it is back.

skylands citadel up

The citadel

Serves the browser. It shows every island it can reach, carries your answers to the right machine, and holds none of your code: what you read comes from the island while you look.

skylands devworld up

Both, on one machine

Runs the island and the citadel together in one process. It is the way to start, and the way to work when one machine is all you need.
